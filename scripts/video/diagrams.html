What it never claims

Observed, inferred, not tested. And “unknown” is never a pass.

ObservedThe browser did it and recorded the result: a page it opened, a button it pressed.
InferredA link exists on the page, but the scan did not follow it.
Not testedSkipped for safety, permission or budget. Nothing is claimed about it.
Not recheckedA page that could not be looked at again is listed as unknown, never as fixed.

Automated checks start the conversation. Human testing finishes it.
