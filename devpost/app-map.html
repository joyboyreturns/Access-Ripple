A short route through the code

1. Follow a real scan

Start at web/src/App.tsx → run(). It creates a scan, follows progress, then loads the versioned graph. web/src/api.ts owns HTTP requests, timeouts, rescan, comparison, and export.

2. Keep the evidence honest

src/scan/worker.ts collects real browser evidence. src/graph/build-graph.ts labels observed, inferred, and untested relationships. No arbitrary external control is activated.

3. Make the evidence understandable

web/src/presentation.ts groups repeated findings without losing member IDs and targets. web/src/GraphCanvas.tsx projects the scene, and App.tsx provides the complete searchable list and readable inspector.

An optional practice to reuse

Add a plain-language explanation for another accessibility rule in presentation.ts. Confirm that its original rule and targets remain available in Developer evidence, and that an unknown rule still falls back to its recorded label.

This practices a useful distinction: improve how evidence is presented without changing what the scanner observed.
